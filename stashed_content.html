Daniel Lee

Daniel is a deacon with BCC, and a pioneer member who responded to the call to be part of BCC's church planting vision.

Daniel has worked for more than 30 years in the civil service – starting out as an organisational psychologist and serving later in corporate administration and more recently, as a director overseeing a portfolio in service delivery.

He is married to Charlotte with two grown-up children Isaac and Hannah. He has a passion for community service and actively contributes his time to 5miles - a non profit organisation that ministers to under-privileged communities - and the Towdah orphanage in Bintan that was established by his wife in 2015.
